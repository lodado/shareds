# Workflow F — GEO / AI Search Readiness

## Scope

Assess whether content can be discovered, understood, and trusted, not a secret AI ranking formula. Identify the specific engine/surface; Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, and third-party assistants do not have interchangeable crawler controls or reporting. Apply Google rules to Google; verify other providers' official documentation before provider-specific advice.

## Readiness review

For each representative URL record observed evidence, gap, proposed improvement, confidence, and validation:

- Crawl/index access and snippet eligibility; distinguish live headers, source intent, and actual indexing evidence.
- Clear headings and logical structure, with a direct answer to the real question where useful.
- Original verifiable information: first-hand testing, data, examples, methods, constraints; never invented experience.
- Identifiable authors/organization, accountable ownership, and consistent brand/product facts.
- Claims linked to original sources and clear limitations; current facts and meaningful update practices.
- Important information in accessible text, rather than only an image/canvas or an unavailable interaction.
- Useful internal links and discoverable supporting pages; relevant images/video with text context.
- Structured data fitting the visible content, not AI-only schema or exaggerated claims.

Use “ready within observed scope,” “gap,” or “unknown” per criterion. Do not generate a pseudo-official GEO score or turn unknown into failure. Prioritize verified accessibility problems over speculative formatting tricks.

## Google guidance and controls

[AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), checked 2026-10-28, applies normal SEO fundamentals to AI Overviews/AI Mode. Supporting pages need indexing and snippet eligibility; technical eligibility does not guarantee selection. Check crawl access, internal links, text availability, useful media and page experience. Do not equate successful URL inspection with an AI citation.

[Google's generative AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), updated 2026-07-10 and accessed 2026-10-28, favors original, useful content and ordinary technical SEO. It rejects AI-specific text files, artificial chunking, and inauthentic mentions as Google visibility shortcuts. No special schema is required. Do not recommend bulk FAQs or rewriting everything for AI. Other services' use of llms.txt requires separate evidence.

The current guide also refers to inclusion controls for Search generative AI features in Search Console. Verify the current official control documentation and the property's actual setting if accessible; otherwise mark it unknown. Never toggle it as part of an audit. Keep indexing, snippets, feature-inclusion settings, and other providers' training controls conceptually separate.

## Measurement and uncertainty

Route actual performance analysis to [measurement](measurement.md). Use available first-party generative-AI reports only within their metric/dimension definitions. A captured answer citing a URL is a dated observation for that query/surface, not market-wide visibility. Repeatable sample studies need a stated query set, market, dates, account conditions, and denominator; do not extrapolate them into total traffic or causal uplift.

AI referral sessions, observed citations, and Google report impressions measure different things. A referrer can be missing, and an impression is not a click or conversion. Without data, propose a measurement method and label expected benefit `[Hypothesis]`.
