# Coverage gaps — areas this skill has no standard for

Work in these areas still follows the request, edit, critique, and delivery contracts. Only the
design decision itself has no rule here.

- Data-dense tables and grids: column priority, row density, sort and filter controls, row actions.
- Charts and data visualization: chart choice, series color, axes and legends.
- Right-to-left layouts and bidirectional text.
- Screen-reader reading flow and live-region announcements, beyond accessible names and focus order.
- Legal, pricing, consent, and policy wording: its owner writes it; never draft it as product copy.

When a request lands here:

1. Say in the brief and in the delivery report that this skill has no standard for it.
2. Use the highest available source in the SKILL.md design authority order and cite it.
3. Record the choice as an `agent proposal` in the Reference Log, not as a rule of this skill.

Legal, pricing, consent, and policy wording is the exception: leave a marked placeholder and name
its owner instead of proposing text.

A gap closes only through the promotion rules in [asset-learning.md](asset-learning.md) or a
user-approved rule in [product-copy.md](product-copy.md). Delete its line here when it closes.
