# Motion and timing

Starting values for an adult house cat; retime from your own video references.

## Idle life (independent timers, layered)

- Breathing: about 2–3 s per breath at rest, 3–4 s and deeper asleep; the chest and belly rise, not the whole
  cat.
- Ear flick: one ear, 0.08–0.15 s out and back, at random every 3–15 s.
- Blink: rare and irregular, seconds to tens of seconds apart; a full blink takes 0.1–0.15 s; half-blinks and
  narrowing when relaxed.
- Tail-tip twitch: 0.2–0.4 s, every few seconds when alert or focused.
- Look: head turn in 0.15–0.3 s, then a still hold of 0.6–2 s; the ear on that side moved first.

## Gaits

- Walk: lateral sequence LH → LF → RH → RF, quarter-cycle offsets, two or three feet down, calm cycle
  0.8–1.2 s. Walking faster shortens the stance (foot on the ground) while the swing stays about the same. The
  head stays steady while the body bobs slightly under it, and the shoulder blades roll.
- Stalk: the walk slowed down, with pauses mid-step (a paw held up), body low, head forward.
- Trot: diagonal pairs (LH with RF, RH with LF), cycle 0.4–0.6 s.
- Bounding run: the spine gathers and extends, with a flight phase, cycle about 0.3 s; the tail streams out as
  a counterweight; the head moves less than the body.
- Stylized short legs may move less than real ones, but keep the footfall order and the steady head.

## Big moves

- Anticipation → action → follow-through. Pounce: crouch with wiggle 0.3–1.5 s, launch ≤ 0.1 s, 0.3–0.5 s in
  the air with the front paws reaching, land front first, squash about 0.1 s, recover.
- Startle: reaction ≤ 0.12 s (a few frames), a flinch or hop, freeze 0.3–1 s.
- Swat: out 0.1–0.2 s, back 0.2–0.3 s.

## Follow-through and easing

- The tail follows the body 0.1–0.25 s late and overshoots; the head overshoots a turn slightly and settles;
  the ears settle last.
- Voluntary moves start fast and land precisely (ease out); the body never drifts.
- Stillness between moves is part of the motion; do not fill every second.

## Variation

- Vary durations by 20–30 %, draw from variants, keep the ears and blinks out of sync.
- Seed the random source so a test can replay a session.

## Reduced motion

- Keep states and signals as static poses: ears, lids, tail position, body height.
- Drop continuous oscillation (breathing amplitude, sway, purr jitter); replace leaps, flinches and pounce arcs
  with short crossfades or snaps.
