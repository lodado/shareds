# Body language channels

Show a mood as a set of channels that agree. One channel moving alone reads as a glitch; channels that disagree
read as a mood in transition (purring while the tail lashes means the cat is about to stop enjoying it).

## Channels

- **Ears** (each ear on its own; fast, 0.08–0.15 s per change): forward and slightly out = relaxed or
  interested; upright, forward and swiveling = alert; rotated sideways = irritated or uneasy; flattened back
  against the head = fear or defense; one ear turned = listening. A flick is a quick twitch and return.
- **Eyes** (lids and pupils): soft, half-closed lids and slow blinks = relaxed, trusting; wide open with dilated
  pupils = excited by play, or afraid; narrow pupils with a hard, unblinking stare = focus or challenge;
  closed = asleep or content. Gaze shifts are mostly head turns; the eyes alone move only a little.
- **Tail** (slow, lags the body): straight up, tip may hook = friendly greeting; low-to-mid with a gentle curve
  and slow sway = calm; low and straight with a twitching tip = focused, hunting; swish → lash or thump =
  irritation rising; puffed like a bottle brush = fear or startle; wrapped around the paws when sitting =
  settled; tucked under = fear. An upright tail that quivers = an excited greeting.
- **Body**: relaxed sprawl or belly up = trust, not an invitation to touch the belly; loaf = calm but ready; low
  and tense = hunting or fear (ears and eyes tell which); arched, side-on and puffed = defensive threat; leaning
  forward = interest.
- **Mouth and whiskers**: whiskers forward = interest, hunting; flat back = fear. Mouth open: yawn (relaxed),
  hiss (fear, defense), a brief open-mouthed sniff after a strong smell.
- **Fur**: skin rippling along the back = irritation from touch; raised fur = fear.
- **Sound**: purring is a low rumble around 20–30 Hz, usually contentment (it can also mean stress, so read the
  other channels); a short meow greets or asks; a hiss defends.

## Mood presets

Two axes, valence (unhappy ↔ content) and arousal (calm ↔ alert), plus energy:

- content, calm: ears neutral, lids half closed, pupils normal, tail relaxed or wrapped, purring allowed.
- content, alert (greeting, play invitation): ears forward, eyes open, tail up, body raised.
- interested (watching prey): ears forward, pupils dilated, tail low with a twitching tip, body low.
- irritated: ears sideways, tail swish → lash, skin ripple, pupils dilating, head toward the source.
- afraid: ears flat, pupils fully round, body low or arched, tail tucked or puffed, mouth may hiss.
- sleepy: lids heavy, slow breathing, ears still but flicking at sounds, tail wrapped.

## Intensity steps

Go through the steps in order instead of jumping to the end:

- tail: tip flick → swish → lash or thump
- ears: slightly out → sideways → flat
- pupils: normal → dilated → fully round
- body: relaxed → still → stiff
