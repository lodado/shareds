# Conditional discovery closure

Read only when its trigger applies, not at every author invocation. In Delivery with a model package,
after IMPLEMENTED_GREEN and again before the final report, on every runtime anomaly or escape, and when
writing requirement inventory, fault model, metamorphic relations, operator dispositions or discovery
decisions or dispatching an AI operator, read [`discovery.md`](../discovery.md) with dependencies.
Use scripts/oracle-discovery.mjs close to attack the declared space. Its verdict and residual-risk list
are evidence, not a delivery state, policy approval or a second orchestration process.

Re-read current locked package, sources, snapshot and evidence. Do not edit locked model/package/card
policy to make closure pass. Return candidates, verdict, residual risks and actual evidence identities
to the controller. Outcome-changing findings require a human decision and new revision where applicable.
Source interpretations do not become approved behavior merely because a discovery operator emitted them.
An author cannot approve or activate their own candidate in the same run.

Re-read [`mandatory-verification.md`](../mandatory-verification.md) and
[`bend-cross-verification.md`](../bend-cross-verification.md) before verification/GREEN/review.
Closure replaces none of their proof, product correspondence, compiler or sampling obligations.
Only ledger-bound executions justify execution claims. A missing or failed observation is not PASS.
Return existing evidence/candidate artifacts only, never new mandatory artifacts, receipts or transitions.

After the lock only, never while writing the card, read
[`card/retro-metrics.md`](../card/retro-metrics.md) for any defect found after lock, run metrics at
REVIEW_VERIFIED, and conditional guardrail-candidate review of reusable execution observations after
immediate existing feedback routing. These are not gates and Low loads applicable review nodes too.
Escape classes and residual limits remain those of case-space and retro-metrics, not a claim that declared
space equals reality. Neither a discovery verdict nor a scanner observation certifies visual evidence.
