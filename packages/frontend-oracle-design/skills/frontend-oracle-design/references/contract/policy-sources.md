# Source interpretation and problem-definition review

Use [common](../common.md) source priority/jurisdiction and approved Source Registry identities.
Accepted policy is actual user answers, approved exact-version specs/designs, mandatory constraints
and designated public compatibility/API/architecture contracts. Agent advice, observed implementation,
existing tests, browser observations and reviewer preferences are evidence, never policy authority.
Map every decided outcome to its source. Conflicts, inaccessible required standards or unclear
jurisdiction require controller decision, never silent averaging or lower-source override.

Before an uncertain purpose, cross-boundary impact, unexplained failure, material dismissal, conflicting
evidence or repeated escape, investigate the actual user task, intended outcome, approved goal, harm,
alternative hypotheses and bounded checked scope. Reuse [lifecycle adaptation](../lifecycle-adaptation.md).
Missing rows do not establish NON_ORACLE_OPINION. Source-backed omitted requirements are POLICY_GAP.
Record candidates in existing journal, not new policy or a fabricated defect classification.
Conditional source-aware fresh review compares verbatim answers/approved excerpts to every affected
decision, linked row, question or justified non-application. It supplements, not replaces blind reviews.
Retain original evidence, judgment, residual uncertainty and reopening conditions for dismissals.
Promote findings only through actual human approval/new revision. Sweep interactions at authoring,
never let discovery candidates or current behavior silently determine expected outcomes.
